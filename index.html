Privacy Policy

Hiddenville: Hidden Objects · Effective October 28, 2026

This policy explains what information the Hiddenville: Hidden Objects mobile game (the “App”) collects, why, and the choices you have. The App is published by Ahmad Mauz (“we”, “us”). By using the App you agree to this policy.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, photos or contacts.
  • Your progress, stars, hints and settings are stored on your device.
  • We use Google services (AdMob, Firebase, Google Play) to show ads, understand how the game is played, fix crashes and process purchases. These services receive device identifiers and usage data.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before ads can be personalised.
  • Notifications are optional and only used if you allow them.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

None, unless you choose to email us. The App has no sign-up, login or profile. If you contact us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

Information stored only on your device

Your game progress (levels finished, stars, the level in progress), your hint and coin balances, purchases you have unlocked, and your settings (sound, music, vibration, reminders) are saved in the App’s private storage on your device. We do not upload this to our own servers. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes it.

Information collected automatically

When you use the App, the services described in section 2 automatically collect:

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, screen size, App version.
  • Identifiers: the Android Advertising ID (or Apple IDFA where allowed), Firebase installation and app-instance IDs, and a push notification token if you allow notifications.
  • Gameplay events: for example starting or finishing a level, using a hint, watching a rewarded video or opening the shop. These are not linked to your name.
  • Approximate location: country or region inferred from your IP address. We never request precise (GPS) location.
  • Crash and performance data: stack traces and device state at the moment of a crash or error.
  • Ad interaction data: which ads were shown, viewed or tapped.
  • Purchase information: which item was bought and a purchase token from Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.

2. Third-party services

The App relies on these services, each operated by Google under its own privacy terms:

Google AdMob Ads

Shows video and full-screen ads. Uses the advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions to deliver, measure and (where you have consented) personalise ads, and to prevent fraud. Uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to ask for consent where required.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services

Firebase Analytics Analytics

Tells us how the game is played as a whole (for example, how many players finish world 1 or where they get stuck) so we can improve levels and difficulty.

Firebase Crashlytics Stability

Sends a report when the App crashes or hits an error so we can find and fix the problem.

Firebase Remote Config Settings

Lets us adjust game settings (such as hint pack sizes or ad timing) and announce new worlds without an App update. It uses an app-instance identifier.

Firebase Cloud Messaging Optional

Delivers occasional announcements, such as a new world being ready, only if you allow notifications.

Google Play Billing Purchases

Processes in-app purchases. Google handles your payment; we only receive confirmation of what was bought.

Read more in the Google Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone except these service providers.

3. How we use information

  • To run the game and keep your progress, hints and purchases working.
  • To show ads, including rewarded videos you choose to watch for hints or coins.
  • To understand how players use the App and make levels, difficulty and features better.
  • To find and fix crashes and bugs.
  • To deliver and restore in-app purchases.
  • To send notifications you have agreed to receive.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to meet legal obligations.

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, our legal bases are: your consent (personalised ads and notifications), performance of a contract (providing the game and purchases you buy), and our legitimate interests (analytics, crash reporting, security and non-personalised ads).

4. Advertising and consent

The App is free and supported by ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, a consent message from Google appears so you can choose whether your data may be used for personalised ads. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads, which use limited data for things like frequency capping and fraud prevention.

Ads in the App are limited to content rated suitable for teens and older. If you buy Remove Ads, full-screen ads are no longer loaded; rewarded videos remain available only if you choose to watch them.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, at any time in your device settings (on Android: Settings › Google › Ads; on iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking).

5. In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play (or the Apple App Store on iOS). Your payment details are collected and kept by them, not by us. The App receives a purchase confirmation so it can grant your item and restore it if you reinstall. Refunds are handled under the store’s refund policy.

6. Notifications

The App only asks for permission to send notifications when you choose a reminder, never at launch. Daily reminders are scheduled on your device and do not send any data. If you allow notifications, a push token is created by Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can send occasional announcements to all players. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

8. Data retention

  • On your device: kept until you clear the App’s data or uninstall it.
  • Analytics: event data is kept for no longer than 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports: kept for up to 90 days.
  • Advertising data: kept by Google under its own retention policies.
  • Emails you send us: kept only as long as needed to answer you.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California privacy laws), you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information held about you, or get a copy of it;
  • have it corrected or deleted;
  • object to or restrict its processing, or withdraw consent at any time;
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

Because the App has no accounts, we cannot identify you from your email alone. The quickest ways to act are: uninstall the App (deletes everything stored on the device), reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, and turn off notifications. For any other request, email us and we will help, usually within 30 days. We will never discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

10. Children

Hiddenville is a general-audience game for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security and international transfers

Data sent to Google’s services is encrypted in transit. Google may process information in the United States and other countries; where the GDPR applies, such transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we only use reputable providers and collect as little as we can.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App changes or the law requires it. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the App.
